# Caption craft research (10.10.26), basis for CAPTION-ENGINE.md

Confidence key: [V] = source page read in the research session · [P] = practitioner knowledge, not verified, treat as hypothesis to test on our account. No statistics invented.

## 1. The caption's job relative to a Reel
- [V] Mosseri: ranking leans on watch time, likes and sends; sends weigh more for non-followers. "One of the most important signals we use in ranking is sends per reach." Make content people would send to someone they care about; don't force it. socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034 · searchenginejournal.com/instagram-algorithm-shift-why-sends-matter-more-than-ever/521389
- [V] Instagram search reads caption and comment text; plain keywords belong in the caption; hashtags won't hack distribution; public professional posts can appear in Google since July 2025. kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo
- [V] TikTok descriptions up to 2,200 chars; on-screen text and voiceover not indexed, so the written caption is the searchable layer. lindseygamble.com/blog/tiktok-expands-the-character-limit-for-video-descriptions
- [V] Length data thin and conflicting: Social Insider (9.1M posts, 2023) found <30-word captions had higher engagement rate, Reels not separated; Hootsuite 10-Reel test, author says not to generalize. Our own data: length vs reach Spearman −0.16 (n=54). Conclusion: length is a craft decision.
- [P] Truncation ~125 chars commonly cited, not primary-sourced, Hebrew RTL untested. First line = second hook.

## 2. Techniques that make a caption ADD to the video
Test: if a viewer who watched gets nothing new, delete it. Moves [P]: second half of the story · the why behind the video · backstory with a specific detail · the thing the video couldn't show · counterintuitive takeaway · first-person admission (only if real) · say what the viewer is thinking · open loop back to the video (sparingly) · specific answerable question · name who it's for (sends).
- [V] Harry Dry's three tests: can you visualize it, can you falsify it, can nobody else say it. gp.demandcurve.com/lessons/fundamental-rules-of-good-copy
- [V] Schwartz awareness stages: unaware → story/surprise; problem-aware → name the pain, no pitch; solution-aware → how it works; product-aware → proof; most-aware → offer + simple step. stylerave.com/stages-of-awareness-eugene-schwartz
- [P] Voice of customer (Copyhackers): reuse real member phrasing verbatim rather than invented copy.

## 3. StoryBrand in short captions
- [V] Customer = hero, brand = guide (empathy + authority). "Companies tend to sell solutions to external problems, but human beings buy solutions to internal problems." External / internal / philosophical problem. Social advice: one clear want, speak to the internal problem, simple (numbered) plan, one direct CTA with a transitional option, show stakes (life with and without). socialmediaexaminer.com/selling-with-story-how-to-make-your-customer-the-hero-donald-miller
- [P] Adaptation for 60–120 words: lead with ONE SB7 element per caption and rotate; internal problem in the member's words; member is the grammatical subject; plan ≤3 steps; stakes plain, never shaming; identity transformation; never label the structure; check against recent captions for repeated skeletons.

## 4. Anti-AI tells / what reads human
- [V] tropes.fyi/directory: negative parallelism ("not X, it's Y"; "Not X. Not Y. Just Z."), rule of three, fragment chains, em-dash addiction, quotable one-liners, stakes inflation, forced metaphor, "here's the kicker", "imagine a world", false vulnerability, summary/tie-back endings, announce-then-answer, synonym cycling, bold-first bullets.
- [P] Also: rhetorical-question openers, "journey/unlock/elevate/game-changer", moral last line, perfectly balanced sentences, emoji as bullets, every caption ending in a question.
- [P] Human: one specific number/name/place/time, one sensory detail, asymmetric rhythm, opinion someone could disagree with, real first-person admission, local references, unpolished edges.
